Relevant news uses existing market-listings, market-watchlist and market-news APIs, one request each per load. No backend module or percoin news requests are needed. The existing shared RSS service reads two allowlisted publishers, caches for 600 seconds and internally retains at most 50 headlines; market-news returns at most 12 headlines. Browser analysis processes at most 100 rows and copies only title, publisher, URL and publication timestamp.

Exact known catalog IDs from OrbitPerformance.positions with positive quantity establish held relevance. Known saved market-watchlist IDs establish watched relevance. Unmapped positions and absent catalog assets are omitted. Full catalog names are matched with Unicode boundaries; symbols are never used. Names shorter than four characters and conservative ambiguous-name exclusions (Near, Flow, Render, Internet Computer) are omitted. This can miss relevant stories; relevance is not verified token identity or a comprehensive news search.

Controls select all news, holdings, watchlist, exact asset and publisher. Held headlines precede watchlisted headlines, then other headlines; dates sort within priority. Canonical HTTPS URLs remove fragments, trailing slash and common tracking parameters. Normalized headlines also deduplicate. Unsafe schemes, credentials and local/private IPv4 links are rejected. Future dates beyond 60 seconds are discarded; missing/invalid dates display unavailable. Publisher metadata and titles are escaped. Links are read-only and open with noopener/noreferrer.

Run node --test feature_relevant_news.test.cjs. Parent integration must load feature_relevant_news.js before opening its registry entry; OrbitPerformance provides existing portfolio identity mapping.
