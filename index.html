CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

A few things to know.

Does connecting a wallet calculate my profit automatically?

A wallet balance shows holdings, not their original purchase cost. Record or import acquisition trades and sales for weighted-average P&L.

Does Orbit place trades or require my seed phrase?

Orbit is a read-only tracker. Use public wallet addresses and dedicated read-only exchange keys. Never enter a seed phrase or private key.

Are all prices, tokens and historical charts available?

Coverage varies by provider, network and exchange pair. Quotes include source and timing information. Missing data remains unavailable rather than becoming an invented valuation.

Does Chart Studio run any Pine script?

Studio supports saved drawings and a bounded Pine-like indicator subset, including supported EMA, RSI and MACD studies. It does not run the full TradingView Pine runtime or simulate strategy orders.
