Orbit product guide · Wallet connections

Track the address. Choose the right network.

MetaMask and Trust Wallet are wallet applications. Your assets live on specific blockchain networks, and tracking starts with the public address that holds them.

Wallet address or token contract?

Your wallet address identifies the account whose holdings you want to view. A token contract identifies a particular asset on a particular network. Adding a token contract as your wallet address will not show the balance held by your personal account.

When tracking a custom token, add your holding wallet first, select the correct chain, and then use the token contract to identify the asset. Identical tickers on different chains do not necessarily represent the same token.

Why the network matters

An EVM address can have the same text on Ethereum and other compatible chains while holding different balances on each. Select the networks you use rather than assuming that an Ethereum balance covers every chain. Networks such as Bitcoin and Solana use different address formats and data providers.

Why a token or value may be missing

  • The selected network is different from the network holding the asset.
  • The provider supports native balances but cannot discover every token automatically.
  • A custom token needs its contract identity added.
  • The quote provider has no reliable price for that asset.
  • A network or token exists only on a testnet, where balances do not establish real-world value.

A token amount and its market value are different data points. Without a reliable quote, a missing valuation should remain unknown rather than being displayed as a confirmed zero.

History has coverage limits

Transaction history depends on chain support, provider limits and pagination. Transfers and contract calls are not automatically complete purchase records. Review detected swaps before recording trades, and import earlier acquisition history when available.

What information should you provide?

Public address tracking does not require a recovery phrase or private key. Orbit’s browser-wallet connection requests account access when you press its connection button. You can also enter a public address manually.

Public addresses reveal on-chain activity and may link that activity to your identity. Only add addresses you intend to track in your account.
