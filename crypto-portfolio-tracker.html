Built for the details that matter

Wallet balances and trade holdings are shown separately to help avoid double counting. Quotes include their source and timestamp. Missing prices and incomplete history remain visible instead of becoming invented valuations.

Chart Studio supports drawing tools, saved setups and a bounded Pine-like indicator subset. It does not execute the full TradingView Pine runtime or simulate strategy orders. History depth and refresh availability depend on the selected provider.

Start with the right records

  1. Add the public wallet address that holds your assets, then choose its network.
  2. Load available balances and history. A token contract identifies an asset; it is different from your wallet address.
  3. Record or import acquisition trades before relying on portfolio profit calculations.
  4. Review quote timestamps, fees and missing history before making decisions.
