About this product

Clear records. Visible limitations.

Orbit is a personal crypto portfolio workspace for reviewing wallet balances, recorded trades and market research.

What the workspace provides

Orbit brings together public-address tracking, supported exchange and CSV workflows, recorded-trade accounting, watchlists, learning notes and chart tools. An account is required to save and manage a portfolio. Public guides can be read without signing in.

Where data comes from

Market rankings and aggregate coin data use CoinGecko where available. Exchange chart sources include Binance and Coinbase Exchange for supported mapped products. Chain balance and transaction coverage varies by network adapter. Provider availability, request limits and listing history affect what can be shown.

USD and USDT are distinct quote units. Chart ranges reflect available observations and are not synthetic history. Supply snapshots are not token unlock schedules; missing protocol revenue or unlock data should not be treated as zero.

Portfolio accounting

Recorded trades use weighted-average acquisition cost in USD. Connected wallet balances are separated from recorded-trade holdings. Public transfers alone do not prove acquisition price, ownership transfers or tax treatment. See the P&L methodology and worked example.

Charts and alerts

Chart studies and drawings assist research. The Pine editor supports a bounded subset of indicator expressions, not the full TradingView runtime, strategy backtesting or broker execution. Price alerts are currently observed in-app when evaluated; email, SMS and background delivery are not provided.

Security and responsible use

Public-address tracking does not require wallet recovery phrases or private keys. Account access is authenticated, but users should still consider the privacy implications of associating public wallet activity with an account. This product does not promise investment returns or provide individualized investment advice.
